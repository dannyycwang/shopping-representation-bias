830   7.2    Scope of the Visibility Claim                                                   888
831   Visibility means eligibility at an offline retrieval cutoff. It is not                 889
832   observed exposure, clicks, satisfaction, purchase, or welfare, and                     890
833   human relevance labels are not objective product quality. The                          891
834   shopping-agent motivation explains why candidate inclusion mat-                        892
835   ters but does not replace an agent evaluation. The mechanism may                       893
836   arise wherever unordered records are serialized for neural retrieval,                  894
837   including jobs, hotels, restaurants, and events; neither its existence                 895
838   nor its magnitude in those domains is established here.                                896

      The evidence covers two English public datasets, synthetic fact-

      preserving permutations, fixed catalogs, and offline exact retrieval.

      ESCI judgments are incomplete and the historical union differs

      from its official small candidate pools. New query IDs were frozen

      before evaluation, but the source dataset and historical test results

      had already been accessed. Query holdout is not unseen-product or

      unseen-category generalization. Empty query prefixes retain the
848                                                                                          906
      historical model condition; optimized prompts, retraining, ANN

      indexing, commercial search, and live users remain untested. Rank

      attention mechanism. No non-inferiority margin or broad method-
