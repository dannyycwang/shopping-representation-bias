\begin{itemize}
\item We characterize attribute-order sensitivity at the level of relevant-product candidate inclusion, using catalog-wide and target-only interventions and distinguishing persistent inclusion, cutoff crossing, and persistent omission.
\item Across two benchmarks and three frozen retrievers, we find inclusion changes with fixed competitors and without target truncation. We relate these changes to Recall and graded effectiveness, showing when aggregate scores leave product membership unspecified.
