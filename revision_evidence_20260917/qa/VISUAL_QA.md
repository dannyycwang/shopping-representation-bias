# Visual QA

The manuscript was inspected at its original layout, including Sections 1–3. Its Libertine body typography is reused in the schematic and plots.

Final PDF proofs were rendered with Poppler at 150 dpi and inspected at their 7.1-inch manuscript width. Both pages of the membership figure were inspected. The SVGs were also rasterized independently with Sharp/librsvg to verify their vector paths and typography. The final PDFs use embedded TrueType outlines and contain no raster image objects.

The schematic labels fixed raw C0 competitors, intact entry blocks, independent target replacement, and the three requested inclusion rows. Its text is at least 9.5 pt. The membership plots have readable schedule labels, consistent scales, all six contrasts, net-change intervals, and explicit query/pair counts. The fitting figure has unclipped encoder/support labels, a separate count column, and consistent state aggregation.

Earlier CFF-font embedding and clipping defects were corrected before delivery. Only the current final proofs are authoritative. Automated PDF size, vector-content and page-count checks are in `data/validation_report.json`.
