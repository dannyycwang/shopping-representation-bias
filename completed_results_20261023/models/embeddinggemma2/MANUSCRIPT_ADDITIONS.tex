\paragraph{Recent-model follow-up.} We retained the original catalogs, query supports, judgments, and seven attribute schedules. Dedicated model-native preprocessing, pooling, and projection were used with full-dimensional unit-normalized embeddings and exact FP32 ranking. This follow-up was specified after inspecting the historical study results.
Qwen3-Embedding-4B was deferred at the user's request after the EmbeddingGemma 2/ESCI result became available. It has no completed retrieval result in this follow-up. The remaining active-model experiments and canonical comparisons use EmbeddingGemma 2; this scope amendment is documented separately.
For embeddinggemma2 on WANDS, source-order Recall@20 was 0.4188; target-only VI@20 was 10.36\% (95\% query-bootstrap CI [9.12, 11.68]).
For embeddinggemma2 on ESCI, source-order Recall@20 was 0.8186; target-only VI@20 was 1.70\% (95\% query-bootstrap CI [1.21, 2.32]).
