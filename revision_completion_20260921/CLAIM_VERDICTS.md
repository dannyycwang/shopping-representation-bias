## Recommendations to the author for Chapters 1--4 (not applied)

Replace the Section 4.3 opening with: “We include lexical and hybrid retrieval controls to examine the contribution of order-invariant lexical evidence.” Hybrid is not structurally invariant. Narrow the Introduction's stable-aggregate/significant-turnover wording to “Net Recall changes can understate relevant-product membership changes.” Replace the third contribution's asserted performance/instability trade-off with the neutral joint-evaluation contribution above. Explain casefold and exact fallback field rules as supplied in the appendix. No Chapters 1--4 source was edited.
