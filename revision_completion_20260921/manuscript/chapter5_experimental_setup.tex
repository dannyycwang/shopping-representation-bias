\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experimental_setup}

\subsection{Data and Evaluation Populations}
\label{sec:evaluation_populations}

We evaluate WANDS~\cite{chen2022wands} and the US English portion of the small-version ESCI test split~\cite{reddy2022shopping}. WANDS retains its full catalog of 42,994 products. We use the existing split of 96 development and 384 held-out queries; the 308 held-out queries with at least one Exact product contribute 21,299 query--product pairs. ESCI uses the existing deterministic sample of 500 queries and the union of their judged products, yielding a fixed catalog of 10,076 products. Its 499 queries with at least one E-labeled product contribute 4,434 pairs. Every primary strategy comparison within a dataset retains the same catalog and eligible query population; fully-fitting diagnostics restrict targets only. These are evaluations with incomplete relevance judgments, not estimates of access to every truly relevant product.

WANDS attributes are the complete pipe-separated entries in its product-features field. ESCI attributes are the nonempty brand, color, and complete bullet-point fields; the bullet-point field is retained as one entry. ESCI therefore has at most three entries per product. Titles, descriptions, and other non-attribute fields retain their text and section placement. Duplicate attribute occurrences are preserved. Data preparation removes duplicate judgments and excludes contradictory WANDS query--product labels.

\subsection{Encoders and Ordering Schedules}
\label{sec:encoders_schedules}

The sensitivity and pure-canonicalization experiments use frozen \texttt{all-\allowbreak MiniLM-\allowbreak L6-\allowbreak v2}, \texttt{bge-\allowbreak base-\allowbreak en-\allowbreak v1.5}, and \texttt{gte-\allowbreak modernbert-\allowbreak base}, abbreviated MiniLM, BGE, and GTE. Their input limits are 256, 512, and 8,192 tokens, respectively; MiniLM uses attention-mask mean pooling, and BGE and GTE use CLS pooling. We retain empty query prefixes and unit-normalized embeddings. Retrieval uses exact FP32 dot-product scores and resolves ties by ascending fixed catalog index. Pinned revisions and execution details accompany the artifacts.

The seven schedules are source order (C0), reverse attribute order (C1), and five product-dependent shuffles (C2s1--C2s5), using seeds 20260911--20260915. Both interventions follow Section~3. Fully-fitting eligibility requires every complete input, including special tokens, to fit the corresponding encoder limit under all seven schedules; only evaluated targets are filtered, while all competitors remain.

The three canonical rules are fixed and all are reported. Aggregation and hybrid comparisons cover BGE and MiniLM on both datasets. Full-record aggregation uses $M=2$ for this common comparison; the existing BGE sweep additionally covers $M=4,7$. Its view seeds, 20260971--20260977, differ from the evaluation-schedule seeds. Set-Mean uses equal weights, BM25 uses $k_1=1.2$ and $b=0.75$, and hybrid retrieval uses RRF with constant 60.

\subsection{Evaluation and Comparison Protocol}
\label{sec:comparison_protocol}

We report query-macro Recall, persistent inclusion, VI, and persistent omission at $K=20,100$, with pair-micro results kept separate. Membership comparisons use the same $H_q$ and distinguish gains, losses, and their net difference. Exact within-query cancellation requires equal positive integer gain and loss counts. Source-order Recall and the raw seven-schedule mean are separate references; target-only inclusion is not labeled catalog-wide Recall.

All common strategy, order, canonicalization, and cutoff analyses use 10,000 paired query-cluster bootstrap draws with seed 2026091701 and descriptive 95\% percentile intervals. Each draw retains a query's products and compared conditions together. Intervals are uncorrected for multiple comparisons; containing zero does not establish equivalence. The common strategy table reports seven-schedule mean Recall and VI. Paired intervals, source-order comparisons, membership counts, and persistent states are supplied separately. Hybrid uses the complete 1-based dense and BM25 ranks for each schedule; its C0 results exactly reproduce the saved source-order hybrid. Historical embeddings are reused without re-encoding, and reconstructed highest-label raw ranks agree with the authoritative saved ranks in all 28 hybrid conditions. Historical strategy selection used WANDS development queries, whereas the three pure canonical rules were fixed before their additional retrieval runs. Earlier outcomes had already been inspected; these analyses are not a newly untouched confirmation set, and no test-set winner is selected. Cost distinguishes historical offline cache construction, query encoding, exact scoring/sorting, and dense/sparse array bytes. Cache-hit elapsed times are not cold-build costs; local exact retrieval timings are not ANN deployment measurements. Additional diagnostics use K=20,50,100,200,500,1000 on fixed target support within each curve.
