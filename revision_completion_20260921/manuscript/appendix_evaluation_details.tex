Lexical canonicalization sorts each complete attribute entry by the tuple
$(\texttt{entry.casefold()},\texttt{entry})$, ascending or descending as specified.
The type/style rule extracts the text before the first colon and lowercases it;
without a colon, it uses the first 40 characters instead. Entries whose extracted
text contains \texttt{producttype}, \texttt{type}, or \texttt{style} are placed first.
Each group uses ascending lexical order with the same tuple. Duplicate occurrences
remain. Unkeyed entries receive no invented field names. Historical template-based
canonicalization changes text framing and is excluded from the pure-sorting comparisons.

For each shuffle seed and product identifier, the first eight bytes of
SHA256 applied to \texttt{seed:product\_id}, interpreted as a big-endian integer,
seed Python's random shuffle. Raw schedules start from source order; full-record
aggregation starts from the canonical ascending list and uses its separate seed
sequence. We retain repeated views, use the requested $M$ with equal weight, and
record distinct texts and vectors separately. In ESCI, the complete bullet-point
field is a single entry; with brand and color, the maximum number of distinct
entry permutations is six.

MiniLM, BGE, and GTE use pinned revision prefixes
\texttt{1110a2\allowbreak 43fdf4}, \texttt{a5beb1\allowbreak e3e68b}, and \texttt{e7f32e\allowbreak 3c00f9},
respectively. Full repository identifiers and revisions are in the frozen protocol.
CUDA encoding uses FP16 model weights and FP32 pooling and normalization.
Historical query embeddings and authoritative saved ranks are reused.
Independent floating-point encoder executions are not assumed bitwise identical.

Fitting eligibility uses untruncated token counts including special tokens for
every one of the seven inputs. Table~\ref{tab:fitting_support} gives the restricted
supports. Retrieval keeps the complete original catalog. The illustrative
WANDS query \emph{teal chair}, product 24318, has ranks 11 and 939 under C0 and C2s5
with 42,993 competitors fixed. Its seven inputs each contain 1,059 tokens under
GTE's 8,192-token limit. This post-hoc example does not estimate typical rank displacement.

The historical WANDS split orders query identifiers by SHA256 of
\texttt{phase3-dev:query\_id}, retaining 96 development and 384 held-out identifiers.
ESCI selects 500 query identifiers by SHA256 of \texttt{20260904:query\_id} from
the US small-version test split. Highest-label-free queries are excluded from
Recall and inclusion summaries. The historical development-selected appearance
rule is distinct from the fixed type/style rule reported here. BGE's $M=4,7$
results retain their existing fixed-budget comparison role; a held-out maximum
is not presented as a tuned deployment choice.

All completion-package intervals, including BGE view-budget contrasts, use
10,000 paired query-cluster draws (seed 2026091701). Historical seed-20260907
intervals are not used in the common main or supplementary strategy tables. Existing cost measurements use an Intel i7-13700H CPU
and RTX 4060 Laptop GPU with 8 GB memory. Exact CPU scoring uses four threads,
the first 50 queries, one warm-up, and five measured repetitions. Reported array
storage excludes vocabulary, metadata, and approximate-search overhead.
Cached encoding time is not treated as a matched cold-build benchmark.

Target-only severity uses best and worst ranks over the same seven schedules.
A crossing satisfies $\mathrm{best}\leq K<\mathrm{worst}$.
All-target severe-crossing shares divide by every product in $H_q$ before query
averaging. Conditional query-macro shares first divide by a query's crossing
count and then average only queries with crossings. Pair-micro conditional
fractions weight crossing pairs equally. The artifact also reports the ratio
of query-normalized severe and crossing mass, which is a different weighting.
Source-order-directed losses additionally require C0 inclusion and omission in
at least one alternative schedule. ECDFs and rank-range distributions retain
both full and fully-fitting supports; the complete competitor catalog remains.
